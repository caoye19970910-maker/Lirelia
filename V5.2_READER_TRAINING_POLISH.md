# Lirelia Mobile V5.2 — Reader & Training Polish

V5.2 focuses on the ten real-use issues reported after V5.1.

## Training
- Noun gender questions are choice-only: tap masculine or feminine; no `un/une` typing step.
- Wrong questions are stored per training session, visible during the session, on a paused session card, and after the last completed session.

## Reading typography
- Font size range is expanded from 14–34 to 14–64 in both the Readium EPUB reader and the classic text reader.
- Line-height range is expanded to 90–260%, with sliders as well as +/- controls.
- Readium disables publisher typography overrides when the reader changes font/size/line spacing/margins so adjustments actually take effect.
- “书籍衬线” is clarified as “系统衬线” and is forced separately from original-book typography.

## Sentence segmentation & translation
- TXT/PDF sentence extraction now uses a French locale-aware BreakIterator and merges common abbreviation fragments.
- EPUB sentence extraction uses `Intl.Segmenter('fr')` when available, with an abbreviation-aware fallback.
- Sentence translation is Google-first (`fr → zh-CN`), with MyMemory only as a network fallback.

## Annotation & vocabulary radar
- Primary highlight colors are darker and easier to see.
- Later occurrences of a learned/highlighted word use a deliberately lighter color.
- EPUB repeated-word paint is re-applied across the Readium settling window to survive DOM rebuilds after pagination/style/decorations.

## Line following
- EPUB Aa adds an independent `双线行跟随` switch with adjustable vertical position, band height, top-line color and bottom-line color. The area between the lines is softly tinted.
- The classic reader’s existing reading ruler now has a stronger double-line mode with two independently selectable colors and a pale band between them.

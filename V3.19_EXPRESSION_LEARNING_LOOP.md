# Language Reader Mobile V3.19 — Expression Learning Loop

## What changed

- Smart phrases detected around a tapped word can now be saved directly with **+ 表达**.
- Saved expressions keep Chinese meaning, source sentence, book id/title, and multiple later contexts.
- French Training adds **我的表达** as a dedicated mode.
- Daily mixed training can schedule saved expressions together with gender, prepositions, conjugation, sentence output, and personal vocabulary.
- Expression questions prefer a cloze built from the user's real reading sentence; otherwise they fall back to meaning -> French active recall.
- Personal vocabulary reviews inside French Training now synchronize their next due time from `AdaptiveTrainingStore`, removing the previous competing fixed 1/3/7/14/30-day schedule on that path.
- Expression data is included in backup/restore.

## Deliberately unchanged

- Dictionary entries still show all available meanings.
- V3.18 reading profile and manual network/voice route remain intact.
- Existing vocabulary/highlight databases migrate in-place; LearningStore schema upgrades from 9 to 10.

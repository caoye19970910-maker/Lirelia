# V3.16 Word Card & Vocabulary Radar

## Reader vocabulary marking
- Yellow / green / blue / pink word highlight automatically adds the selected word to the vocabulary bank and marks it as learning.
- The chosen color is stored with the vocabulary item.
- Every occurrence of the same normalized French word in all books is rendered with a much paler version of that color while the item remains in learning status.
- Clearing the colored mark from the source selection removes that vocabulary entry and removes global pale reminders.
- Marking a word as known stops the global pale reminder.

## Local underline
- `U` is a separate underline annotation.
- Underline does not add the word to vocabulary and does not propagate to other occurrences/books.

## Dictionary card
- Quick marking and mastery controls are placed near the top.
- Sentence translation is directly below Chinese definitions.
- Related expressions and word-family/related words display Chinese meanings; local dictionary lookup is tried first, with the existing free translation fallback when allowed.

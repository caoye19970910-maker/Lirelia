# Lirelia Mobile V4.9 — Reading Magnifier + Full-screen Vocabulary Review

This pass responds to the 2026-09-23 real-device feedback after V4.8.

## 1. Reading helper redesign

The V4.8 dark ruler band and on-page drag/resize handles have been removed from the Readium EPUB path.

V4.9 uses a persistent magnifier-style reading lens instead:

- no black band over the text;
- no floating `⋮` / `↕` handles on the reading page;
- the lens follows a configurable vertical reading position;
- lens width and height are configurable;
- magnification is configurable from 110% to 220%;
- all controls live under the EPUB `Aa` typography sheet;
- settings are persisted through `AppSettings`.

The lens is temporarily hidden while the `Aa` sheet itself is open so it cannot cover the controls. It reappears immediately with the latest values when the sheet closes.

Implementation uses the platform magnifier on supported Android versions, with the Readium navigator view as the magnified source.

## 2. Vocabulary review redesign

The old centered review dialog has been replaced with a full-screen review surface.

- larger headword area;
- answer/context section can scroll independently on small screens;
- `显示答案` and rating actions stay at the bottom;
- an `自动发音` switch is available in the review screen;
- the switch is persistent;
- when enabled, each newly displayed review word is spoken automatically;
- the manual `🔊 发音` button remains available.

## Compatibility

The existing V4.8 annotation/highlight work and V4.7 dictionary-context fixes are retained. Existing ruler preference keys are reused where practical so upgrades do not discard the user's saved position/size.
